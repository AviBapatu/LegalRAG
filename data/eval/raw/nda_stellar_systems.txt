Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is between "Stellar Systems Inc." and "Nova Labs" entered into to support a potential manufacturing joint venture.

1. Confidential Information
1.1 "Confidential Information" includes trade secrets, technical data, product plans, customers, markets, software, developments, and engineering information.
1.2 This information may be disclosed in written, oral, or visual form, whether or not marked as confidential.

2. Obligations
2.1 Each party will hold the other party's Confidential Information in confidence using at least reasonable care.
2.2 Each party will use the information only to evaluate the manufacturing joint venture.

3. Term
3.1 This Agreement continues for two years unless earlier terminated.
3.2 Upon termination, all Confidential Information is returned or destroyed.
3.3 For this manufacturing joint venture engagement, confidentiality remains in effect for three years after the Effective Date.
