# Employment Agreement

This Employment Agreement (the "Agreement") is made by and between Blue Sky
Software Inc. ("Employer") and Jordan Lee ("Employee").

## Section 1. Position

1.1 The Employer agrees to employ the Employee as Senior Engineer.

1.2 The Employee shall report to the Chief Technology Officer.

## Section 2. Compensation

2.1 The Employee shall receive an annual base salary of one hundred twenty
thousand dollars.

2.2 The Employee shall be eligible for an annual discretionary bonus.

## Section 3. Term

3.1 This Agreement shall commence on the first business day of the calendar
year.

3.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement upon thirty days written notice.
